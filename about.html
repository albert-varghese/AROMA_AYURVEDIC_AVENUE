About Us

Traditional Ayurveda, Personalized Care and Holistic Wellness

Aroma Ayurvedic Avenue is a Kerala Ayurvedic wellness centre in Vazhoor, Kottayam, dedicated to traditional Ayurvedic care and holistic wellbeing. With 40+ years of experience, our approach combines traditional knowledge, personalized attention and a calm natural setting.

Ayurveda considers more than a single treatment. Guests can explore traditional therapies, wellness activities, medicinal plant experiences, lifestyle guidance and comfortable accommodation as part of a peaceful wellness stay in Kerala.
